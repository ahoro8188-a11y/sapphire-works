Welcome

Hi, I'm Maya —
the eye behind Sapphire Works.

I've spent the last several years chasing golden hour across coastlines and cobblestone towns, learning that the best wedding photographs are rarely posed — they're caught mid-laugh, mid-vow, mid-glance.

My approach is quiet and observational. I stay close enough to catch the details, far enough to let your day unfold exactly as it should. No forced poses, no interrupted moments — just the two of you, as you actually are.
